# Intro video: how it is made

ITRI626 SU5–7 · checklist item *"System documentation: intro video"*.

A screen recording with **on-screen captions and an AI voice-over**: the narration is generated by
**Kokoro-82M**, an open-source text-to-speech model (Apache 2.0) running locally on the GPU, in three voices,
one per part of the video, each with its own caption colour (below); the closing line gets a robot effect as
a "yes, this was AI" ending. Lines are written with joining words instead of commas, which the voice reads
more naturally.

| Voice | Speaks | Caption colour |
|---|---|---|
| `am_puck` | title, the hub (shots 1–2), the repo (9), closing | pale yellow |
| `af_heart` | the local model (shots 3–4) and the fine-tune results (8) | light blue |
| `am_michael` | Dispatch (shots 5–7) | orange |
Finished video: `out/mission-control-intro.mp4`.

## How it is made
1. **Record** each shot below as its own short clip with KDE's **Spectacle**
   (*New Screen Recording → Full Screen*; stop with the Spectacle icon in the panel). Record them in order.
2. **Move the clips** into [`clips/`](clips/) (Spectacle saves to `~/Videos` by default). Their date-time names
   keep them in recording order. Retake a shot by deleting its clip and recording it again.
3. **Narration** (only when the text changes): `distrobox enter gpu -- ~/.venvs/tts/bin/python voices.py`
   writes `narration/title.wav`, `1.wav` … `end.wav` (about 15 s on the GPU). The text is `NARRATION` in
   [`voices.py`](voices.py), written as it should be spoken (numbers in words).
4. **Build:** `python3 make_video.py`. It burns in the captions, adds each part's narration (holding the last
   frame if a clip is shorter than its line; pauses after sentences and colons are added in `voices.py`), speeds up waiting, adds a title and end
   card, and joins everything into `out/mission-control-intro.mp4`. It also writes `out/contact-sheet.png`
   (one frame per shot) to check every caption at a glance.

Captions, speed-ups and trims are in the `SHOTS` list at the top of [`make_video.py`](make_video.py). Only
ffmpeg is used (already on the host); raw clips are not committed.

## Before recording
- Terminal: full screen, **large font** (Ctrl + `+` a few times in Konsole) so it reads on a projector.
- Ollama **stopped** (so shot 3 shows it starting): `systemctl --user stop ollama`.
- Have `models/router-finetune/results/eval.md` ready to show for shot 8 (in a terminal: `cat`, or the README
  on GitHub).
- Nothing private on screen (other windows, notifications).
